About Yukon Self Storage

Located at 500 Maxi Dr, Yukon, OK 73099, Yukon Self Storage is a locally owned and operated facility built around convenience, security, and fair pricing. We understand that choosing a storage provider is about trust, and we take that seriously.

Here is what sets us apart:

  • Drive-Up Access — Pull your vehicle right up to your unit door for easy loading and unloading. No elevators, no hallways, no hassle.
  • 24/7 Keypad Entry — Access your belongings on your schedule, any time of day or night, with our secure electronic keypad system.
  • Video Monitoring — Our facility is equipped with surveillance cameras to help keep your stored items safe around the clock.
  • Month-to-Month Rentals — No long-term contracts required. Rent for as long as you need and cancel when you are done.

Unit Sizes to Fit Every Need

We offer a range of unit sizes to accommodate everything from a few boxes to a full household of furniture:

  • 5x10 Units — Ideal for seasonal items, small furniture pieces, or a few dozen boxes. Think of it as a walk-in closet.
  • 10x10 Units — Our most popular size, fitting the contents of a one-bedroom apartment. Great for a mid-sized move or business inventory.
  • 10x20 Units — Perfect for larger moves or when you need to store the contents of a multi-bedroom home, including appliances and large furniture.

Not sure which size is right for you? Check out our Size Guide for detailed comparisons, or give us a call and we will help you figure it out.
